Wake word ONNX models
=====================

melspectrogram.onnx, embedding_model.onnx, alexa_v0.1.onnx,
hey_jarvis_v0.1.onnx, hey_mycroft_v0.1.onnx, and hey_rhasspy_v0.1.onnx
are from the openWakeWord v0.5.1 release by David Scripka
(https://github.com/dscripka/openWakeWord), Apache License 2.0.

ok_nabu.onnx is the community openWakeWord “Okay Nabu” classifier used
by Home Assistant voice satellites (same embedding pipeline).
